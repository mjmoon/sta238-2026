Simple Linear Regression Model

A simple linear regression model for bivariate data \((x_1,y_1),\) \((x_2,y_2),\) …, \((x_n,y_n)\) assumes that \(x_i\)’s are non-random and that \(y_i\)’s are realizations of random variables \(Y_i\)’s for \(i=1,...,n\) satisfying

\[Y_i=\beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + U_i\]

for \(i=1,2,...,n\) where \(U_i\)’s are independent random variables with \(\mathbb{E}(U_i)=0\) and \(\text{Var}(U_i)=\sigma^2<\infty\) for \(i=1,2,...,n\).

Model

\[Y_i=\beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + U_i\]

with \(\mathbb{E}(U_i)=0\) and \(\text{Var}(U_i)<\infty\).


Regression line

\[y=\beta_0 + \beta_1 x\]

  • \(\beta_0\) is the intercept

  • \(\beta_1\) is the slope

  • \(x\) is the independent variable or explanatory variable

  • \(y\) is the dependent variable or response variable

  • One way to fit such a line is to find a line that minimizes the error between the line and the observed \(y_i\)s.
  • The vertical differences between the fitted line and the observed \(y_i\)’s are called the residuals.

Method of least squares

\[y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x\]

Goal: To estimate \(\beta_0\) and \(\beta_1\) such that the resulting fitted line minimizes the sum of squared residuals.

Method of least squares

\[y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x\]

Goal: To estimate \(\beta_0\) and \(\beta_1\) such that the resulting fitted line minimizes the sum of squared residuals.

That is, find \(b_0\) and \(b_1\) such that they minimize

\[g(b_0,\beta_1)=\sum_{i=1}^n \left[y_i - \left(b_0 + b_1 x_i\right)\right]^2\]

\[\frac{\partial}{\partial b_0}g(b_0,b_1)= \sum_{i=1}^n 2\left[y_i\left(b_0+b_1 x_i\right)\right](-1) \overset{set}\equiv0\] \[\frac{\partial}{\partial b_1}g(b_0,b_1) =\sum_{i=1}^n 2\left[y_i-\left(b_0+b_1 x_i\right)\right](-x_i) \overset{set}\equiv0\]

\[b^*_1 =\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\overline{x}_n)(y_i-\overline{y}_n)}{ \sum_{i=1}^n (x_i-\overline{x})^2}\quad \text{and}\quad b^*_0=\overline{y}_n - b^*_1 \overline{x}_n\] \[\text{minimize }g(b_0,b_1).\]

Method of least squares

The assumptions \(\mathbb{E}(U_i)=0\) and \(\text{Var}(U_i)=\sigma^2<\infty\) for all \(i=1,2,...,n\) must hold for the method to provide valid estimates.

The method can be expanded to models with more independent variables based on the same principle of minimizing the sum of squares for residuals.

The method of least squares for a simple linear regression models

\[Y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + U_i\]

for \(i=1,2,...,n\) estimates the regression line with least squares estimates,

\[\widehat{\beta}_1 =\frac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}\quad \text{and}\quad \widehat{\beta}_0=\overline{y}_n - \widehat{\beta}_1 \overline{x}_n\]

where

  • \(S_{xy}=\sum_{i=1}^n(x_i-\overline{x}_n)(y_i-\overline{y}_n)\), and
  • \(S_{xx}=\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i-\overline{x})^2\)

based on observed data \((x_1,y_1)\), \((x_2,y_2)\), …, \((x_n,y_n)\). The estimated regression line is

\[y=\widehat{\beta}_0 + \widehat{\beta}_1 x.\]
